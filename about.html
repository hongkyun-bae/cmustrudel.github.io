About

Dr. Hong-Kyun Bae is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Kookmin University. He leads the ARIS Lab (Applied Recommendation and Information Systems Lab), where he conducts research in artificial intelligence, recommender systems, information retrieval, and learning-to-rank.

Research Interests

  • Recommender Systems and Information Retrieval
  • Model Efficiency and Lightweight Architectures
  • Multi-objective Optimization in Ranking
  • Learning-to-Rank (LTR) Algorithms
